![Portrait of Saskia Kroonenberg](/assets/picture-saskia-in-the-sun.jpeg)

Saskia Kroonenberg is a visiting fellow at [inherit. heritage in transformation](https://www.inherit.hu-berlin.de/fellows/saskia-kroonenberg)  (HU Berlin) and a lecturer in [cultural studies at Radboud University](https://www.ru.nl/en/people/kroonenberg-s) in the Netherlands. She was previously a Marie-Curie fellow at [the a.r.t.e.s. Graduate School for the Humanities](https://artes.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/41302.html) (University of Cologne), where she wrote a PhD on Antonio Gramsci's political philosophy, postcolonial literature and new materialism. It was graded summa cum laude and won the [Antonio Gramsci dissertation prize 2023](https://wien.arbeiterkammer.at/interessenvertretung/arbeitundsoziales/Antonio_Gramsci_Preis/index.html).

Saskia has [published](https://saskia.dance/academic/) in various journals and is currently finalizing a monograph about the notion of the mother tongue in contemporary Italy. She has been a visiting researcher at the [Royal Netherlands Institute](https://www.knir.it/en/) in Rome, the [Erich Auerbach Institute for Advanced Studies](https://auerbach-institut.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/en/fellows-and-hosts/current-fellows-hosts/saskia-kroonenberg-hanna-zehschnetzler) in Cologne, the [University of Zurich](https://www.rose.uzh.ch/de.html) and [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/institute-for-cultural-inquiry). She sometimes writes blogs for [Atria](https://atria.nl/en) and is a board member of Radboud's [Halkes Women+ Faculty Network](https://www.ru.nl/en/departments/interdisciplinair/halkes-women-faculty-network). She also loves to dance.
