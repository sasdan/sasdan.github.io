Saskia Kroonenberg is a scholar in cultural studies at [Radboud University](https://www.ru.nl/en/people/kroonenberg-s) in the Netherlands. She was previously a Marie-Curie fellow at the [a.r.t.e.s. Graduate School](https://artes.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/41302.html) for the Humanities in Cologne. Her PhD combined Antonio Gramsci's political philosophy with postcolonial literature and new materialism. It was graded summa cum laude and won the [Antonio Gramsci prize 2023](https://wien.arbeiterkammer.at/interessenvertretung/arbeitundsoziales/Antonio_Gramsci_Preis/index.html).

Saskia has been a visiting researcher at [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/institute-for-cultural-inquiry), the [University of Zurich](https://www.rose.uzh.ch/de.html), the [Royal Netherlands Institute in Rome](https://www.knir.it/en/) and the [Erich Auerbach Institute for Advanced Studies](https://auerbach-institut.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/en/fellows-and-hosts/current-fellows-hosts/saskia-kroonenberg-hanna-zehschnetzler) in Cologne. She is currently preparing a monograph at Amsterdam University Press about the notion of the mother tongue in contemporary Italy. She also loves to dance.
