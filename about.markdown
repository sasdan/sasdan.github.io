Saskia Kroonenberg is a scholar in literary studies at the [Department of Modern Languages and Cultures](https://www.ru.nl/en/people/kroonenberg-s) of Radboud University. She was a Marie-Curie fellow at the [a.r.t.e.s. Graduate School for the Humanities](https://artes.phil-fak.uni-koeln.de/41302.html) in Cologne. Her PhD combined Antonio Gramsci's political philosophy with postcolonial literature and new materialism, and was graded summa cum laude.  

Saskia has been a guest researcher at [Utrecht University](https://www.uu.nl/en/research/institute-for-cultural-inquiry) and at the [University of Zurich](https://www.rose.uzh.ch/de.html). She is currently preparing a monograph at Amsterdam University Press about the notion of the mother tongue in contemporary Italy. She also loves to dance.
